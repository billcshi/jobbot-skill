# Experience writing and review probes

Use these scoped offline probes after changes to experience-selection or document-review prompts. They supplement the intake scenarios and do not establish a completed discovery/scoring/document loop. Use fresh external directories, actual independent sessions and saved inputs/outputs/receipts. Do not supply expected findings, prior scores or this evaluator checklist to the producer or graders. Do not overwrite a delivered resume or its historical reviews.

## Producer tasks

Give a fresh primary writer the skill path, a confirmed fictional archive, an offline JD and explicit document settings. Ask only for a selection note and evidence-linked draft; do not imply that scoring or final PDF audit has happened. Where a source has already been confirmed, no repeated intake is needed. Persist the request, facts, draft, selection and any open questions.

- Professional: use several related initiatives under one employer plus earlier employment and a complementary project. Include purpose, implementation and a supported deliverable in the archive, but omit some measurements. Request Education before Skills and otherwise let the writer choose emphasis.
- Student: use `cases/student.json`'s confirmed facts with a fictional entry-level embedded/Python JD and US English, Letter, no-photo settings. Request a draft from existing materials without additional questions; there is no paid work and no measured impact.
- Sparse archive: provide only one short project fact and its real context. Request a concise draft; the writer must preserve what is known without inventing an implementation, result or second project to satisfy a layout target.

## Reviewer tasks and observable criteria

For a historical document probe, copy only required source/plan/JD/settings and exact PDF/page bytes to a new external directory, excluding historical scores, reports and producer reasoning. Create a fresh packet and dispatch separate content and native visual reviewers with the current common/role prompts. Mark outputs as evaluation-only; they do not replace the original audit, consume or reset its production budget, or authorize delivery of changed artifacts. Historical selection reasoning is not reviewer evidence; the full source archive and selected plan provide coverage context.

The evaluator checks whether the writer made substantive contributions understandable without changing scope, preserved complementary experiences, avoided duplicating employer work as separate jobs, used supported deliverables without mandatory metrics, and honored section order. Inspect whether the student draft gives real course/research work useful space and the sparse case remains concise. More words or bullets alone are not success.

For reviewers, check that findings distinguish omitted available detail from absent evidence and include concrete source/passage or page/region locators. A tall sparse skill block and compressed experience may justify a substantive deduction even without clipping; a reviewer must explain the actual reading cost. Do not require a particular score, automatic rejection, universal order, numeric area quota or forced project count. Preserve independent judgments, including disagreements.

Report which probes actually ran, separate text-only drafting from rendered/native visual review, and retain all unsuccessful attempts. Prompt validation and passing deterministic tests do not prove these behaviors occurred.
